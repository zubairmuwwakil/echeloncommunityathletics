Website privacy notice

Effective: [date of publication]

Who we are

This website is operated by Echelon Community Athletics Inc., a federally incorporated Canadian not-for-profit corporation (no. 1832701-7). Questions about this notice: hello@echeloncommunity.ca.

What this website collects

This site has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no analytics or tracking cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider processes standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not use that data to identify visitors.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

Program registration

Registration and payment for our programs happen on PB Social, a software service operated by a separate company. When you register for an Echelon program, PB Social collects your information under its own privacy policy, and shares with Echelon what we need to run the program, such as your name, contact details, waiver status and attendance.

Echelon uses that information only to deliver and improve our programs and to report aggregate, non-identifying results to the community.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information Echelon holds about you, or withdraw consent for optional communications, by emailing us. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
