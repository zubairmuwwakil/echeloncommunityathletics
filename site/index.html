Community pickleball · Durham Region, Ontario

More than a game. A place to belong.

Echelon Community Athletics is a not-for-profit that runs welcoming, coach-led pickleball programs. Come as a complete beginner, leave with a game you love and people to play it with.

See programs and register ↗ Our mission ↓

Programs are organized by Echelon Community Athletics Inc. Online registration and payment run on PB Social, a separate software service.

  • Beginner-first — no experience needed
  • Coach-led by Pickleball Canada certified coaches
  • Equipment provided for learn-to-play
  • Not-for-profit, federally incorporated

Our purpose

Sport should feel open to everyone.

We organize community athletic and sports games, starting with pickleball: one of the easiest sports to pick up and one of the most social to keep playing.

Echelon Community Athletics Inc. promotes physical health, active living, and community connection through accessible recreational sports programs.

Mission statement

  • 01

    Welcoming by design

    Sessions are built for people who have never held a paddle, with structure that makes the first night easy.

  • 02

    Good coaching, not just court time

    Every learn-to-play night pairs instruction with real games, so skills stick.

  • 03

    Neighbours, not strangers

    Small groups and the same faces each week turn a drop-in into a community.

Learn-to-play leagues

Multi-week beginner leagues that take you from first serve to confident rallies.

  • Paddles and balls provided

Keep playing

Community leagues

Structured weekly play that mixes friendly competition with the social side of the sport, for players ready to play more often.

Skill clinics, special sessions and events that bring players from different programs together.

Hour one

Structured coaching

Hour two

Fun match play

Put it to work in real games with players at your level.

Who we are

Community-led. Accountable.

Echelon is a not-for-profit corporation. Program revenue goes back into programs, and our articles say that if Echelon ever winds down, remaining assets go to qualified donees, never to individuals.

Relationship disclosure

Echelon's founding director also leads Zemi Echelon Inc., the company that builds PB Social. The two are separate legal entities. Any agreement between them is disclosed and decided under Echelon's conflict-of-interest process, with the interested director not voting.
